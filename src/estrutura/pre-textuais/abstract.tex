\begin{resumo}[ABSTRACT]
\begin{SingleSpacing}

The purpose of this study is to develop and propose a technological solution, realized in a mobile application, for the registration of fishing activities, encompassing the location of capture spots, the species of fish and the type of bait utilized. The research was motivated by the difficult found in the routine of fishers in localizing fishing areas that conciliate productivity, safety (low risk of accidents) and accessibility. The digital environment FishSpot was created to fill that gap, offering direct support functionalities to fishers. As a result, a functional prototype capable of centralizing crucial data to fishing activity was obtained. It is concluded that the application represents a tool with significant potential to promote engagement and mutual cooperation in the fishing community, optimizing practices and resource management.

\hfill \break
\textbf{Keywords}: Fishing. Digital Platform. Mobile Application. Software. Development.

\end{SingleSpacing}
\end{resumo}
